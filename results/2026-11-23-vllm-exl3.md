## Tuning on our card (6 repetitions each, mean ± sd, `bench/vllm_bench.py`)

Card power cap here is **230 W** (`hwmon power1_cap`); the recipe's published numbers use 180 W.

| Config | Prefill 12K | Prefill 48K | Decode code | Decode prose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: recipe default (`reference` prefill, DSpark up to 7 proposals) | 1,726 | 1,799 | 86.1 ± 12.6 | 65.5 ± 1.3 |
| B: `INKLING2_PREFILL_PROFILE=optimized` | 2,361 | 2,636 | 88.6 ± 4.6 | 64.4 ± 1.5 |
| C: B + `--max-num-batched-tokens 4096` | 2,280 | 2,661 | 84.1 ± 13.3 | 64.4 ± 0.9 |
| B + `INKLING2_DRAFT_MAX=4` | — | — | 86.7 ± 8.6 | 69.2 ± 1.0 |
| B + `INKLING2_DRAFT_MAX=3` | — | — | 97.2 ± 5.7 | 70.2 ± 0.9 |
| B + `INKLING2_DRAFT_MAX=2` | — | — | 93.4 ± 3.8 | 73.9 ± 1.0 |
| **Final: B + `INKLING2_DRAFT_MAX=3`** | **2,472** | **2,618** | **92.2 ± 4.5** | **71.2 ± 1.1** |

- The `optimized` prefill profile is the recipe author's (opt-in, documented); on this card it gives **+43–46 %**
  prefill. The `--max-num-batched-tokens 4096` tip does not add to it (that profile already batches 6,144).
- **Fewer DSpark proposals decode faster here** (+7–9 % vs the default of 7, with lower variance). The default of 7
  was tuned for multi-turn agent tasks on a 180 W card. Measure on your own workload before changing it.
- Temperature 1.0 makes DSpark acceptance (and code decode speed) noisy: use ≥ 6 repetitions to compare configs.

GPU under load: 52–69 °C, GT0 at 2800 MHz (max), `throttle/reasons = none`. Prefix reuse with opencode works
(cumulative hit rate rose to 62 % during a real session). With one sequence, concurrent clients queue: benchmarks
run next to a live session need outlier filtering (`bench/vllm_bench.py` now uses the median and drops bursts).
